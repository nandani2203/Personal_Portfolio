About Me

Inside My World

A brief introduction to who I am, what I build, and what drives me every day.

Nandani Gupta
Software Developer

I'm drawn to the gap between "it runs" and "it's actually correct" — and every system I've touched so far has had one somewhere. Duplicated logic quietly turning into an audit risk. A cache layer that can drift from the source of truth without anyone noticing until it's too late. A pipeline reprocessing the same data on loop, burning compute for no reason. Different systems, same instinct: find where the invisible failure lives, and rebuild it so it can't happen again.

In just over a year, that instinct has taken me across encryption architecture, distributed billing systems, RAG and retrieval infra, and applied ML with explainability built in — not by staying in one lane, but by chasing the same underlying problem wherever it shows up: systems that look fine on the surface and aren't, underneath.

That's what I want to bring to a team. Not just code that ships, but a habit of catching the failure mode before it finds you.
